# How Do You Approach Problem Solving?
## System
Given a task, I first look for the key words.
Then I research the key words.
Again, I look for the most important parts and find what's useful and how it connects to my initial task.
I write this down, then I evaluate what it means.
Then I make a plan. 
At last I start building the plan.

## Example
For example, I am looking for a job in my field:
Cybersecurity and software development.
I was told resumés were scanned by something called ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems).
Therefore, I researched ATS and found that more than 75 % of resumés are never read by a human (https://studentcv.no/blog/ats-sjekk-cv-guide).
Shocking.
Hours, days and late nights wasted because of this simple system no one had told me about.
